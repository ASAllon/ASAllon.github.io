Privacy policy: KidsGetEDU Channel Manager

Last updated 24 September 2026

KidsGetEDU Channel Manager ("the tool") is used only by KidsGetEDU to manage its own YouTube channel. The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it we are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and our data is handled under the Google Privacy Policy.

What we access

Only our own channel's data, which the channel owner granted through Google sign-in (OAuth). That means channel details, our own videos' metadata, captions and playlists, and our channel's aggregated analytics: daily views, watch time, average view percentage, subscribers gained and lost, likes, estimated revenue and top countries; how viewers found our videos (traffic sources), on which kind of device they watched, and in which YouTube app (YouTube, YouTube Kids, YouTube Music); and for each of our videos, its audience retention, public view count, length and upload time. All of these are totals for our own videos, never data about a single viewer. We do not access other users' data. We do not collect personal data about viewers, and certainly not about children.

How we use it

To publish and describe our own videos (titles, descriptions, translations, captions, thumbnails, playlists) and to measure how they perform.

Storage

On our own computer, in a database that only we can reach. OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest. We keep only the last 28 days of analytics: the daily numbers of those days, and for each video one set of totals over them. They are refreshed from YouTube every night, and older days are deleted in the same step. If one report cannot be read one night, the previous night's numbers stay until the next successful refresh. The one date we keep per video is the first day it was watched in YouTube Kids, read again from YouTube every night.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any YouTube data to third parties.

Revoking access

The channel owner can stop the tool's access at any time in two ways:

  • The tool's Disconnect button deletes the stored token and all stored analytics immediately.
  • Revoking at myaccount.google.com/permissions does the same at the next nightly sync (within 24 hours), when YouTube reports that access is gone.
